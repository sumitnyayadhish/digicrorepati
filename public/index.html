    Free Digital Marketing & AI Quiz – Mock Test Online

    DigiCrorepati is a free, game-style digital marketing quiz and AI quiz built for students, job seekers, freelancers and working marketers in India. It works like a mock test for digital marketing course exams, certification practice (Google Ads, Google Analytics 4, Meta) and digital marketing interview questions — but it's played on a quiz-show prize ladder.

    How the 10 levels work

    • Levels 1–3 (Rookie → Practitioner): SEO basics, CTR, CTA, organic vs paid traffic, GA4 engagement rate, UTM tags, ROAS, what an LLM is, tokens, prompts and hallucinations.
    • Levels 4–6 (Specialist → Expert): Core Web Vitals (INP), canonical and hreflang tags, email deliverability (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), Smart Bidding, E-E-A-T, RAG, embeddings, context windows, prompt injection and MCP.
    • Levels 7–10 (Master → Legend): Conversions API, incrementality testing, Marketing Mix Modeling, Consent Mode v2, India's DPDP Act, LoRA, quantization, KV cache, speculative decoding and real business situation questions.

    Explanations in English, Hinglish and Marathi

    Every answer — right or wrong — comes with a short explanation, so you learn while you play. Switch between English, Hinglish (Hindi in Roman letters) and Marathi (मराठी) any time. It's one of the few Marathi digital marketing quizzes online.
