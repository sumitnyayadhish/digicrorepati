    Free Digital Marketing & AI Quiz – 1,000+ Questions, Mock Test Online

    DigiCrorepati is a free, game-style digital marketing quiz and AI quiz for students, job seekers, freelancers and working marketers in India. Use it as a digital marketing mock test, for Google Ads and GA4 certification practice, or to prepare for digital marketing interview questions — all on a quiz-show prize ladder.

    How the 10 levels work

    • Levels 1–3 (Rookie → Practitioner): SEO basics, CTR, CTA, organic vs paid, GA4 engagement, UTM tags, ROAS, what an LLM is, tokens, prompts and hallucinations.
    • Levels 4–6 (Specialist → Expert): Core Web Vitals, canonical & hreflang, email deliverability, Smart Bidding, Meta Ads, E-E-A-T, RAG, embeddings and prompt injection.
    • Levels 7–10 (Master → Legend): Conversions API, incrementality, MMM, Consent Mode, DPDP Act, unit economics, LoRA, quantization, KV cache and hard situation questions.

    Marketing maths with unlimited practice

    Practise the calculations interviewers love — CTR, CPC, CPM, CPA, ROAS, ROI, break-even ROAS, LTV:CAC, CAC payback and ad budget planning. Every maths question uses fresh numbers, so you never run out.

    Daily challenge, friend challenges and certificates

    Play the 10-question Daily Challenge to build a streak, challenge a friend to the exact same questions, and download a free certificate for every level you clear.

    Explanations in English, Hinglish and Marathi

    Every answer comes with a short explanation. Switch between English, Hinglish and Marathi (मराठी) any time — one of the few Marathi digital marketing quizzes online.
